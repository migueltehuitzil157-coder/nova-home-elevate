# Nova Home Elevate

I want you to recreate and improve my existing website: novasolarwater.com.

Use my current website as the primary design reference. I really like the current overall design, visual quality, layout, typography, spacing, colors, animations, and premium feel. Do NOT completely redesign the website or change its identity.

The goal is to make a refined version of the existing website with a few strategic changes, while making it more professional, polished, high-investment, and optimized to convert visitors into leads.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

The current website already looks good. Preserve the things that work.

I want the final website to feel like a company that invested a significant amount of money into its branding and website.

Think:

 Premium

 Established

 Modern

 High-end

 Professional

 Trustworthy

 Clean

 Sophisticated

 Local New Jersey company

 Not like a cheap contractor website

 Not like a generic AI-generated website

 Not like a basic landing page

Do not make the website look overly salesy or cluttered.

The website should still have the same sophisticated visual style as the current website.

1. HERO SECTION

The current main heading says:

"Powering the homes of New Jersey."

I want this heading changed.

Use:

"Elevating New Jersey Homes."

Keep the heading visually large, premium, and impactful.

Keep the supporting text professional and focused on Nova providing complete home solutions.

The hero should immediately communicate that Nova is more than just a solar company.

Nova provides:

Solar • Roofing • Water 

Do not make this look like four unrelated businesses. Present them as complete solutions for the modern New Jersey home.

2. HERO BUTTONS

The current hero has two buttons:

Explore Solar

and

Meet Nova

I want these changed.

The primary goal of the website is to generate leads, so the buttons should encourage visitors to take action.

Use:

Primary button:

Get a Free Estimate

Secondary button:

Explore Our Services

The primary button should be visually stronger.

When someone clicks Get a Free Estimate, take them directly to the lead/contact form.

The form should be easy to find and should not require users to search through the website.

3. ADD MORE LEAD-GENERATION CTAs

This is very important.

I want the website to have multiple natural opportunities for a visitor to become a lead.

Do NOT simply put the same giant button everywhere.

Instead, strategically place CTA buttons throughout the website.

Examples:

 Hero → Get a Free Estimate

 Solar section → See If Your Home Qualifies

 Roofing section → Get a Roofing Estimate

 Water section → Get a Free Water Assessment

 Services section → Get Started

 Near the bottom → Request Your Free Estimate

 Navigation → Get a Free Estimate

Each CTA should feel natural to the section it belongs to.

The visitor should always have an easy path toward contacting Nova.

4. LEAD FORM

Make the lead form feel premium and trustworthy, not like a cheap generic contact form.

The form should be designed to capture leads for Nova's home services.

Possible fields:

 First Name

 Last Name

 Phone

 Email

 ZIP Code

 What service are you interested in?

 Solar

 Roofing

 Water

Keep the form relatively short so visitors actually complete it.

The primary form CTA should say:

Get My Free Estimate

The form should visually match the rest of the website.

5. REMOVE THE "04 - HOME" SECTION

Remove the section that currently says:

"04 - Home"

and the project/content associated with that section.

I do not want this section on the new website.

Make sure removing it does not leave awkward spacing or break the flow of the page.

Rebalance the sections so the website still feels intentional and professionally designed.

6. REMOVE "BUILT AROUND YOUR HOME"

Remove the section/headline that says:

"Built Around Your Home."

Remove the associated design elements/content if they are specifically part of that section.

Replace the space with something that contributes more directly to the overall Nova brand and lead-generation strategy.

Do not simply leave an empty area.

7. NEW JERSEY MAP / DIAGRAM

There is currently a diagram/map showing New Jersey cities, but the current shape is essentially a random abstract shape.

I want this completely improved.

Make the diagram actually resemble the shape of New Jersey.

It should be immediately recognizable as New Jersey.

Use a clean, sophisticated outline of the state.

The map can include strategically placed New Jersey locations/cities to reinforce that Nova serves customers throughout New Jersey.

For example:

 Newark

 Jersey City

 Paterson

 Elizabeth

Edison

 Trenton

 Princeton

 New Brunswick

 Morristown

 Atlantic City

Do not overcrowd the map.

The map should look intentional, premium, and geographic, not like a random decorative graphic.

The New Jersey outline should be accurate enough that someone immediately recognizes the state.

8. SERVICE POSITIONING

Make sure the website clearly communicates that Nova is a complete home-services company, not only solar.

The primary services should be presented elegantly:

Solar

Clean energy solutions designed around your home.

Roofing

Protection and improvement for one of your home's most important systems.

Water

Better water solutions for your home and family.

Keep the descriptions concise and premium.

9. NAVIGATION

Keep the navigation clean and sophisticated.

Potential navigation:

Home
Solar
Roofing
Water
About
Contact

Add a highly visible CTA on the right:

Get a Free Estimate

The CTA should stand out without looking cheap or overly aggressive.

Make the navigation sticky on desktop and mobile if appropriate.

10. MOBILE RESPONSIVENESS

The entire website must look excellent on:

 Desktop

 Laptop

 Tablet

 iPhone

 Android phones

Pay particular attention to:

 Hero heading size

 Button placement

 Navigation

 Forms

 Map

 Service cards

 Spacing

 Images

 Text wrapping

Do not simply shrink the desktop version.

Create a genuinely polished mobile experience.

12. COPY STYLE

Keep the copy:

 Short

 Confident

 Professional

 Modern

 Human

 Easy to understand

Avoid generic AI phrases and excessive marketing language.

Do not use exaggerated claims such as:

"99% satisfaction"

"best in New Jersey"

"number one"

"guaranteed savings"

unless those claims are actually provided and verified.

The website should feel like a real premium company, not an aggressive lead-generation page.

13. VISUAL DESIGN

Preserve the current visual language of novasolarwater.com.

Keep the existing:

 Color palette

 Premium typography

 Spacing philosophy

 Image treatment

 Animation style

 Section transitions

 Overall sophistication

Improve details where necessary.

Pay special attention to:

 Alignment

 Consistent spacing

 Button styling

 Typography hierarchy

 Image quality

 Mobile responsiveness

 Section transitions

 Visual balance

Everything should feel intentional.

14. DO NOT MAKE IT LOOK LIKE A LANDING PAGE

This is extremely important.

I want this to remain a premium official company website, not a simple Facebook lead-generation landing page.

It should feel like someone could search for Nova, visit the website, and immediately think:

"This is a serious, established home-services company."

Lead generation should be built into the website without sacrificing the premium brand experience.

15. FINAL USER JOURNEY

The ideal visitor journey should be:

Visitor lands on website

↓

Immediately understands Nova provides complete home solutions

↓

Sees a strong CTA

↓

Explores Solar / Roofing / Water

↓

Builds trust through the website

↓

Sees additional CTA opportunities

↓

Clicks Get a Free Estimate

↓

Completes a short, professional form

↓

Becomes a lead

Make this conversion path extremely easy while keeping the website elegant.

FINAL REQUIREMENT

Before finishing, compare the recreated website against novasolarwater.com and make sure the new version still feels like the same company and same premium brand.

Do not unnecessarily change things that already look good.

The goal is:

Keep the beautiful existing design → remove the sections I don't want → improve the NJ map → change the hero → add stronger CTAs → improve lead conversion → maintain a premium, high-investment appearance.

The final result should look like a premium New Jersey home-services company with a serious brand presence, while also functioning as a highly effective lead-generation website.
